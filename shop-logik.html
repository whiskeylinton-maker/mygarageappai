About Shop Logik

Built for shops that run on more than paper tickets

Shop Logik is a shop management and point-of-sale platform for independent auto repair businesses. It brings scheduling, customer records, inventory, and reporting into a single system so shop owners can run the business without stitching together spreadsheets and sticky notes.
